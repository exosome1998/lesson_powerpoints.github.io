BIOLOGY 1 · DNA structure and copying 4 marks

Mc Which base pairs with cytosine? A Adenine B Thymine C Guanine D Cytosine [1]
C — guanine pairs with cytosine.
Short Complete the complementary strand: A–C–T–G–G → ____–____–____–____–____. [1]
T–G–A–C–C.
Explain Explain why DNA base order is important. Start: “The order of bases…” [2]
The order of bases stores genetic instructions, including instructions that influence proteins and characteristics.

BIOLOGY 2 · Genes, alleles and inheritance 5 marks

Mc Which genotype is heterozygous? A BB B Bb C bb D B [1]
B — Bb.
Apply In a simplified model, B is dominant to b. Cross Bb × bb. Complete the four boxes in the diagram. [2]
Bb, bb, Bb, bb (in row order).
Calculate What percentage of offspring is expected to show the recessive trait? Working: ____ of 4 × 100. [2]
2 of 4 × 100 = 50%.

BIOLOGY 3 · Natural selection 5 marks

Mc Natural selection acts on: A acquired needs B inherited variation C all individuals equally D effort [1]
B — inherited variation.
Sequence Put in order: more offspring with trait / environmental selection / inherited variation. [2]
Inherited variation → environmental selection → more offspring with the advantageous trait.
Explain Why might the dark beetles become more common? Use: survival → reproduction → generations. [2]
Better-camouflaged beetles survive more often, reproduce and pass on their alleles; over generations the trait may increase.

BIOLOGY 4 · Evidence of evolution 4 marks

Mc In undisturbed sedimentary strata, fossils in deeper layers are generally: A younger B older C identical age D artificial [1]
B — older.
Compare Identify TWO lines of evidence for common ancestry from the source revision topics. [1]
Fossils, comparative embryology, and/or other shared biological evidence.
Explain How can similarities in early vertebrate embryos support common ancestry? [2]
Shared developmental features can be inherited from a common ancestor.

CHEMISTRY 5 · Conservation and balancing 6 marks

Mc To balance a chemical equation, change: A subscripts B element symbols C coefficients D products [1]
C — coefficients.
Balance __ Mg + __ O₂ → __ MgO (write all three coefficients). [2]
2 Mg + 1 O₂ → 2 MgO.
Balance __ C₃H₈ + __ O₂ → __ CO₂ + __ H₂O [2]
1 C₃H₈ + 5 O₂ → 3 CO₂ + 4 H₂O.
Explain Why must the number of atoms of each element match on both sides? [1]
Atoms rearrange but are conserved in a chemical reaction.

CHEMISTRY 7 · Collision theory and rate 5 marks

Mc Which should react faster, all else equal? A large lumps B fine powder C either equally D no reaction [1]
B — fine powder.
Explain Explain why increasing temperature often increases rate. Sentence starter: “Particles…” [2]
Particles move faster and more collisions have enough energy for a reaction.
Fair Test To compare powdered and lump calcium carbonate reacting with acid, identify the independent variable and ONE controlled variable. [2]
Independent variable: particle size/surface area. Control: mass of carbonate, acid concentration, volume or temperature.

C — rusting steel.
Identify Give TWO observations that can suggest a chemical reaction. [2]
Examples: gas bubbles, precipitate formation, temperature change, colour change.
Evaluate Why is a colour change alone not always conclusive proof? [1]
Physical processes can also change appearance; use additional evidence.

PHYSICS 9 · Speed and acceleration 6 marks

Mc On the speed–time graph, which segment is constant speed? A A B B C C D none [1]
B — segment B.
Calculate A cyclist covers 150 m in 30 s. Speed = distance ÷ time = ____ m/s. [2]
150 ÷ 30 = 5 m/s.
Calculate A scooter changes velocity from 4 to 16 m/s in 3 s. Acceleration = (final − initial) ÷ time. [2]
(16 − 4) ÷ 3 = 4 m/s².
Identify State the SI unit of acceleration. [1]
m/s².

PHYSICS 10 · Balanced and unbalanced forces 5 marks

Mc With zero resultant force, a moving object: A must stop B speeds up C stays at constant velocity D turns [1]
C — stays at constant velocity.
Calculate An 18 N rightward push and 6 N leftward friction act on a trolley. Calculate net force and direction. [2]
18 − 6 = 12 N to the right.
Calculate If the trolley has mass 4 kg, calculate acceleration using F = ma. [2]
a = 12 ÷ 4 = 3 m/s² rightward.

PHYSICS 11 · Wave properties 5 marks

Mc The distance between adjacent crests is: A amplitude B wavelength C frequency D period [1]
B — wavelength.
Calculate Frequency = 8 Hz and wavelength = 1.5 m. Wave speed = frequency × wavelength. [2]
8 × 1.5 = 12 m/s.
Explain Why can sound not travel through a vacuum? [2]
Sound is a mechanical wave and requires particles in a medium to transfer vibrations.

PHYSICS 12 · Reflection and applications 4 marks

Mc For reflection from a plane mirror: A i > r B i < r C i = r D no reflected ray [1]
C — angle of incidence equals angle of reflection.
Identify What is meant by the normal at a surface? [1]
An imaginary line at 90° to the surface.
Explain Explain why light can travel through a vacuum but sound cannot. [2]
Light is an electromagnetic wave; sound is mechanical and requires a medium.

MIXED EXAM 13 · Genetics: apply and justify 5 marks

Mc Two carriers for a recessive condition may have an affected child because: A alleles disappear B both may pass recessive alleles C all children must be affected D the trait is contagious [1]
B — both may pass recessive alleles.
Apply For Rr × Rr, list possible genotypes and the expected recessive proportion. [2]
RR, Rr, Rr, rr; 1 in 4 or 25% recessive.
Explain Why is 25% a probability rather than a guaranteed outcome in four children? [2]
Each child independently inherits alleles; observed results can differ from expected ratios.

MIXED EXAM 14 · Chemistry: explain and calculate 6 marks

Balance __ Al + __ HCl → __ AlCl₃ + __ H₂ [3]
2 Al + 6 HCl → 2 AlCl₃ + 3 H₂.
Predict What observable evidence would show that a gas forms during this reaction? [1]
Bubbling or fizzing (effervescence).
Explain Why should you not change Cl₃ to Cl₂ simply to balance the equation? [2]
Changing subscripts changes the chemical formula and therefore the substance.

MIXED EXAM 15 · Physics: graph reasoning 5 marks

Calculate A vehicle travels at 6 m/s for 12 s. Find distance using the area under a speed–time graph. [2]
6 × 12 = 72 m.
Calculate A vehicle increases speed uniformly from 0 to 10 m/s over 8 s. Find distance using a triangle. [2]
½ × 8 × 10 = 40 m.
Explain Why is the triangular area the distance during uniform acceleration from rest? [1]
Area under a speed–time graph represents distance; a linear increase forms a triangle.

MIXED EXAM 16 · Extended scientific reasoning 6 marks

Evaluate A student says: “Higher temperature always means a reaction is complete sooner.” State one limitation of this claim. [2]
Reaction completion also depends on reactant amounts, other conditions, and whether a reaction is possible; higher temperature generally raises rate, not necessarily completion in every situation.
Explain A beetle population changes colour over generations. Explain why this does not mean individual beetles deliberately changed. [2]
Selection favours pre-existing heritable variants; differential reproduction changes trait frequencies across generations.
Calculate A 3 kg cart experiences a resultant force of 15 N. Show formula, substitution and units. [2]
F = ma; a = F/m = 15/3 = 5 m/s².
