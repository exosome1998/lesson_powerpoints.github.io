UNDERSTAND3. The atmosphere

Fact: The atmosphere is the layer of gases surrounding Earth.
a. Is wind part of the atmosphere?
b. Circle the atmosphere example: wind / ocean / mountain

UNDERSTAND4. The hydrosphere

Fact: The hydrosphere includes all Earth’s water — oceans, rivers, lakes, ice, groundwater and water vapour.
a. Does the hydrosphere include frozen ice?
b. Name one place we find liquid water.

UNDERSTAND5. The lithosphere

Fact: The lithosphere is the solid, rigid rocky outer part of Earth. It includes the rocky seafloor.
a. What is the lithosphere made mainly of?
b. Is rock under the sea part of it?
