Hydrogen molecule, H2. Each H has 1 electron. Add the shared pair using one dot and one cross. [2]
Why is one shared pair enough for each H?
Each hydrogen now counts 2 electrons in its outer shell.

A   sharedB   transferred to ClC   removed from both atoms

Why are the ions attracted?
Opposite charges attract.
Charge on sodium ion:
Na+
Charge on chloride ion:
Cl−
